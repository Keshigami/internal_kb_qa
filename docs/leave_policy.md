# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly from the start date. Part-time employees accrue leave pro rata. Up to 5 unused days roll over into the next year and must be used by 31 March; anything above that is forfeited.

Request leave in the HR system (BambooHR) at least two weeks in advance for absences of three days or more, and at least two working days in advance for shorter absences. Your manager approves leave; a request without a response after five working days is treated as approved.

## Public holidays
The company observes the public holidays of the country in which the employee is contracted. Employees who must work on a public holiday receive a day in lieu to be taken within three months.

## Sick leave
Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per year without a medical certificate. From the fourth consecutive day of absence a doctor's note is required. Longer illness is covered by the company's income protection insurance after 20 working days.

## Parental leave
Primary carers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave; secondary carers receive 6 weeks. Leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months after birth or adoption. Notify HR at least 12 weeks before the intended start date.

## Leave of absence
An unpaid leave of absence of up to six months can be requested for study, caring duties or personal reasons. Submit the leave-of-absence form to HR at least 30 days in advance with your manager's endorsement. The People team confirms within 10 working days. Benefits continue during the first three months; after that the employee pays the employer share.

## Compassionate leave
Up to 5 paid days are available on the death of an immediate family member, and 2 days for a close relative. No form is needed; inform your manager and HR by email.

## Volunteering
Every employee has 2 paid volunteering days per year for registered charities. Log them in BambooHR as "Volunteering".
