# Business Expense Policy

## Purpose
This policy explains how employees submit business expenses for reimbursement and which expenses the company pays for. It applies to all full-time and part-time staff and to contractors whose agreement references it.

## Submitting an expense
Submit every expense through the Expensify app within 30 days of the purchase date. Each claim needs an itemised receipt (a credit-card slip alone is not enough), the business purpose, and the names of any guests. Claims older than 60 days are rejected automatically and need written approval from the finance lead.

Your manager approves claims up to 500 USD. Claims between 500 and 2,000 USD need manager approval and a second approval from the finance lead. Anything above 2,000 USD must be pre-approved in writing before the purchase.

## What is reimbursable
- Travel: economy airfare booked at least 14 days in advance, standard hotel rooms up to 220 USD per night in most cities (320 USD in New York, San Francisco, London and Zurich), ground transport, and 20 USD per day for incidentals.
- Meals while travelling: up to 75 USD per day. Alcohol is reimbursed only at client dinners and capped at two drinks per person.
- Client entertainment: pre-approved by the account owner; the most senior employee present pays and files the claim.
- Home office: one 300 USD stipend per calendar year for a desk, chair or monitor, plus 40 USD per month towards internet.
- Software and subscriptions under 50 USD per month can be expensed; anything more goes through IT procurement.

## What is not reimbursable
Parking or speeding fines, upgrades to business class, mini-bar charges, personal phone plans, clothing, and gifts to colleagues are not reimbursed.

## Reimbursement timing
Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run, so a claim approved by the 20th of the month is paid at the end of that month. Payment goes to the bank account on file in the HR system.

## Corporate cards
Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate card from finance. Card statements are reconciled in Expensify by the 5th of each month; three missed reconciliations lead to the card being suspended.
